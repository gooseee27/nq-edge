    US day session Asia session More trades Both sessions

    The loose rules take extra trades the strict rules skip: at a wall, strong one-sided flow alone is enough (buying at the put wall, selling at the call wall), and price counts as near a level from further away. They have their own trade limit and cooldown, never copy a trade that is already open, and are tracked separately above. A break and retest is when price breaks through a wall, runs at least 20 points past it, comes back to test it and holds, and orderflow does not disagree. All of these numbers are untested guesses.

    Asia starts with a smaller stop and target because overnight moves are smaller. Those numbers are guesses until results come in, which is why the results above are split by session. Asia trades from 8:15 PM to 1:30 AM New York time, Sunday to Thursday evenings. That is 5:15 PM to 10:30 PM in Arizona until Nov 1, then 6:15 PM to 11:30 PM.

    These are fake trades. Yahoo prices run about 10 minutes late, so treat results as a rough guide.

    Phone alerts

    Free and no account. Install the ntfy app on your phone, tap +, and subscribe to the same topic. You get a push when a paper trade opens or closes. Treat the topic like a password. Leave it empty and save to turn alerts off. Alerts come from the 5 minute scan, so they arrive after the trade opens and can lag a few minutes behind.

    Schedule times

    Times are on this computer's clock. Snapshots should land during US market hours: 6:45 AM and 12:30 PM in Arizona until Nov 1, then 7:45 AM and 1:30 PM, because New York changes its clocks and Arizona does not. The scan tasks already cover both. The afternoon snapshot also supplies the overnight gamma levels the Asia session uses.
